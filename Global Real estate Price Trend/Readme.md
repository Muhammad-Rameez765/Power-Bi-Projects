# 🏘️ Global Housing Market Analysis (2015–2024)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **global housing market trends** from 2015 to 2024 using real-world data.
The goal is to understand how housing prices, rents, and affordability have changed over time and how economic factors influence real estate markets.

---

## ❓ Key Questions Answered

**Q: How have housing prices and rents changed globally over time?**
**Q: Is affordability improving or worsening across countries?**
**Q: How do mortgage rates and GDP growth impact housing demand?**

---

## 📊 Dataset

* **Source:** Kaggle – Global Housing Market Analysis (2015–2024)
* **Granularity:** Country-level, yearly data
* **Key Fields:**

  * House Price Index
  * Rent Index
  * Affordability Ratio
  * Mortgage Rate
  * GDP Growth
  * Population Growth
  * Urbanization Rate

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI Desktop
* DAX (Measures & Time Intelligence)
* Data Cleaning & Modeling
* Interactive Dashboards

---

## 🔧 Data Preparation

* Cleaned missing and inconsistent values
* Standardized country names
* Created a Date table for time analysis
* Built calculated measures for YoY growth and affordability trends

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Housing prices increased faster than rents in many countries
* Affordability declined despite economic growth
* Mortgage rates have a strong negative impact on housing demand

---

## 📂 Dashboard Pages

1. **Overview:** Global housing & rent trends
2. **Economic Impact:** GDP, inflation, mortgage rates vs prices
3. **Affordability Analysis:** Country-wise comparison
