## Project Q&A (7 Questions)

Why were support activities treated as resources?
Because direct institutional resource data was unavailable, support mechanisms acted as realistic proxies for allocation analysis.

Why create measures instead of calculated columns?
Measures allow dynamic, filter-aware insights essential for dashboards and stakeholder decisions.

What defines a high-risk student?
Low GPA combined with low parental support and no tutoring.

Why only 16 measures?
Each measure directly answers a business question; additional metrics would add redundancy and confusion.

What is the main driver of poor performance?
Low study time and high absences.

How does tutoring impact GPA?
Students receiving tutoring show consistently higher average GPA.

What decision does this dashboard support?
Targeted academic intervention and optimized support allocation.
